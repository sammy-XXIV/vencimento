The weekly rhythm

From a pile of bills to a plan you can trust.

The agent watches the calendar, not just the balance. It pays in full when it can, waits when it should, and leaves a receipt when money moves.

01 / SEE

Bring the bills together

Enter supplier, amount, due date, grace period, and the wallet that is allowed to receive payment.

02 / DECIDE

Set the owner’s rules

Choose your cash floor, daily cap, and who gets paid first when this week is tighter than expected.

03 / SETTLE

Approve and prove

Review the proposed batch, approve it, and send wBRL on Celo. Every supplier gets a transaction receipt.

The guardrails

Automation with a hand on the till.

Vencimento can rank the bills, but it cannot invent a supplier or quietly spend the reserve. The owner stays in control of the decisions that matter.
